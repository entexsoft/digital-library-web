The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxies

Category: Gaming

Pavel Šibal · 2026

Explore all 256 galaxies of No Man's Sky with this comprehensive atlas of galactic centers. Each galaxy is documented with its name and a verified planetary portal address that brings travelers within a single warp jump of the galactic center, making the journey through the universe easier and more accessible.

Whether you're exploring distant galaxies, establishing a network of portal bases, or searching for discoveries shared by other travelers, this atlas provides a practical reference for navigating the vast universe of No Man's Sky. From Euclid to the final galaxy, it is designed to help explorers reach new destinations and continue their journey across the stars.
